Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

That's the Spot Massage Therapy ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to book and confirm your massage.

Who we are

That's the Spot Massage Therapy is a sole proprietorship owned and operated by George Alvarado, Licensed Massage Therapist (Texas License #MT149213), located at 3765 S Alameda St, Suite 305, Corpus Christi, TX 78411. You can reach us at (361) 373-9592 or georgealvarado2790@yahoo.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the appointment request form on this site, we collect the information you enter, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • The service you're interested in and your preferred day and time
  • Any other details you choose to include in your message

If you call, text, or email us directly, we receive whatever information you share in that message.

Information collected automatically

This is a simple website. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This is used for security and to understand general traffic patterns — it is not used to identify you personally.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Respond to your appointment request and confirm your booking
  • Contact you by phone, text, or email about your appointment
  • Answer questions you send us
  • Keep basic records of appointments and, where you provide it, health or injury information relevant to your massage so your session can be tailored safely

If you provide your phone number in our form, you consent to receive a call or text from us about your appointment request. Message and data rates may apply. We do not send marketing text blasts, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying STOP or telling us directly.

What we do not do

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. Your information stays with us and the service providers below who help us run the business.

Service providers we use

We rely on a few trusted third-party services to operate. When you use them, their own privacy policies also apply:

  • GlossGenius — our online booking and payment platform. If you book or pay online, your booking and payment details are handled by GlossGenius, not stored on this website.
  • FormSubmit — delivers the appointment request form on this site to our email inbox.
  • Google Maps — we embed a map so you can find the studio. Google may collect data as described in its own privacy policy.
  • Our website host — serves the pages of this site.

We do not control these companies' privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies.

Payment information

We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details on this website. All payments are processed securely through GlossGenius or in person at the studio.

Health information

If you share health, injury, or medical information with us so we can tailor your massage, we treat it as confidential and use it only to provide safe and appropriate care. We do not share it with anyone else without your permission, unless required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small amounts of data (for example, to remember that you've already dismissed a pop-up during your visit). You can clear or block this at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep your information

We keep appointment requests and client records only as long as needed to serve you and to meet any recordkeeping requirements that apply to licensed massage therapists in Texas. If you'd like your information removed, just ask.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Correct information that's wrong
  • Request that we delete your information
  • Ask us to stop contacting you

Email georgealvarado2790@yahoo.com or call/text (361) 373-9592 and we'll take care of it.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Minors may only receive massage services with the consent and presence of a parent or legal guardian as required by Texas law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please don't send sensitive information (like a Social Security number) through our website form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
