Good to Know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?+
Yes — That's the Spot is appointment only, so your session is private and unrushed with no waiting around. Booking online is the fastest way to grab a time, or call or text (361) 373-9592.
What are your hours?+
Open 7 days a week, 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Early mornings and late evenings included — the live schedule shows exactly which times are open when you book.
Do you offer a military discount?+
Yes. Active duty and veterans get a military discount — just mention it when you book or let George know at your visit.
What's your cancellation policy?+
Life happens. If something comes up and you can't make your appointment, just let George know ahead of time so the spot can be offered to someone else.
How should I prepare for my massage?+
Come as you are — arrive a few minutes early, wear comfortable clothing, and drink some water before and after. Let George know any focus areas or injuries so he can tailor the session.
How do I pay? Do you offer payment plans?+
Pay securely when you book or at your visit. Flexible Buy Now, Pay Later is available at checkout for eligible bookings.
Is the studio private?+
Yes — it's one client at a time in a calm, private room. No crowded waiting areas, no rush.
